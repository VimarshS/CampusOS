# CampusOS — Milestone Timeline

Estimates assume a solo developer working part-time (~10-15 hrs/week) alongside this mentorship process. Adjust freely — this is a guide, not a deadline imposed on you. We move at your pace; phases are gated by your confirmation, not the calendar.

| Phase | Milestone | Est. Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Project Bootstrap | 2-3 days |
| 1 | Auth & Core Academic Structure | 1 week |
| 2 | Portals Skeleton + Attendance | 1 week |
| 3 | Assignments + Marks & Results | 1.5 weeks |
| 4 | Library + Complaint Management | 1 week |
| 5 | Hostel + Mess Management | 1 week |
| 6 | Placement Portal | 1 week |
| 7 | Events + Communication (real-time) | 1.5 weeks |
| 8 | Analytics Dashboard | 1 week |
| 9 | AI Features (all 8 sub-phases) | 2.5-3 weeks |
| 10 | DevOps Hardening & Deployment | 1 week |
| 11 | Polish & Buffer | 1 week |

**Total: ~14-16 weeks** to a fully deployed, production-grade v1.

## Suggested Milestone Groupings (for resume/portfolio checkpoints)

- **M1 — "Core ERP Alive"** (end of Phase 3): Auth + academics + attendance + assignments + results working end-to-end. Demo-able.
- **M2 — "Campus Life Complete"** (end of Phase 6): Library, hostel, mess, placement all functional.
- **M3 — "Real-Time Campus"** (end of Phase 8): Events, messaging, notifications, analytics live.
- **M4 — "AI-Powered ERP"** (end of Phase 9): All 8 AI features live — this is the differentiator for your portfolio/resume.
- **M5 — "Production Live"** (end of Phase 11): Publicly deployed, monitored, documented.
